## feelings of (un)belonging

i've never felt like i've belonged anywhere, i've always been flawed in so many ways.
and there's always this thought in my head that tells me that i should just quit what i'm doing, and give up.

### the ouroboros

i can't finish up a proper project ever, i burn out and then never touch it cause, *what if i get tired again?*

:::quote
this cycle of starting, burning out, and never finishing is the definition of ouroboros. i feel like i just eat my own tail endlessly, thinking it'll end eventually, but it never does.
:::

maybe i'm meant to be like this, forever chasing an unattainable goal, only to fall back into the same cycle over and over again.

### the facade

on the other hand, i push myself to show a good image of myself for others, but to be fair, *how can i show a great lie without believing in it myself?*

i'm proud of myself of trying, showing. but sometimes i just lose it.

---

## choked desolation

i feel like i'm in a constant **choked desolation**, where i can't breathe but i also don't suffocate. it's a feeling i'd love to understand, but i don't think i ever will.

i feel as if i was the feeling of melancholy. i don't feel sad, though i don't feel happy.

:::note
for some things i'd love to cry, i just can't get myself to.
for some things i'd love to be happy for, i just can't get myself to.
:::

---

## swallowed solitude

i know i've got friends to speak to, or at least people who i can talk to. but i usually feel like i can't.

it'd be amazing to not feel lonely, but i don't know how to fix it. i do have friends, but i have this feel, where i don't have anyone to rely on, to hear me out. i am the one who listens, but doesn't speak—that's why i'm trustful for people.

it's just, i don't want to be relied on, i want to rely on someone as well, but i feel like i'd make them even sadder for carrying my burden on their shoulders as well as theirs.

:::quote
but why don't i use this principle for myself? why do i carry my own burden alongside theirs? it's not like i'm doing a good job at it.
:::

maybe i should start listening to myself more, and rely on myself more.

but something in me says i can't.
